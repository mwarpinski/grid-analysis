## 1. The Core Engine: The "What-If" Sensitivity Matrix

I cannot wait for the ISO to publish the actual Locational Marginal Prices (LMPs). I need your engine to calculate the delta of the **Marginal Cost of Congestion (MCC)** before it clears.

The formula your backend must crunch instantly for every relevant node is:


$$\text{LMP}_{\text{Node}} = \text{LMP}_{\text{System Lambda}} + \text{MCC}_{\text{Node}} + \text{MCL}_{\text{Node}}$$

Where the congestion component is the sum of all binding constraints:


$$\text{MCC}_{\text{Node}} = -\sum_{c \in \text{Constraints}} (\text{Shift Factor}_{\text{Node}, c} \times \text{Shadow Price}_c)$$

### What I need to see:

* **The Exposure Column:** Don't just show me raw Generation Shift Factors (GSFs). Show me **Shift Factor Exposure** ($\text{GSF} \times \text{Shadow Price}$). If a constraint has a massive $2,000 shadow price, even a small 2% shift factor on a node matters immensely to my portfolio.
* **Dynamic Flow Impact Calculator:** Give me a slider or an input field where I can type: *"If I inject an extra 50 MW at Node A and buy 50 MW at Node B, how much does the flow change on Binding Constraint X?"*

## 2. Real-Time Constraint Mapping & Bottleneck Prediction

I need a live visual representation of the grid topology. If a line is running at 98% of its thermal or stability limit, it's a ticking time bomb.

### What I need from the software:

* **Binding vs. Shadowing List:** A clean, auto-refreshing table of every constraint that is currently binding or within 5% of its limit.
* **Penalty Factor Warnings:** If a constraint is violated, I need to see the ISO's absolute ceiling (the Max Shadow Price or Penalty Factor, like $5,000/MW in some regions) so I know exactly how high the local LMP can explode.
* **Directional Flow Vectors:** Clearly indicate which way the power is flowing across a cut-plane or interface so I know which nodes *relieve* the congestion (negative shift factors) and which ones *exacerbate* it (positive shift factors).
